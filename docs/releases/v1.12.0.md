# sgl-node v1.12.0

Set your own price for image, speech and transcription models.

`sgl price set --model <model> --multiplier 1.25` prices a media model at 1.25 times the suggested rate. Allowed range: half to double the suggested rate. `sgl price reset --model <model>` goes back to the suggested rate, and `sgl price show` now lists your media models with their current price. You keep 80% of what the payer is charged.

Prices are not switched on across the network yet. Until they are, the command tells you so and nothing changes. Token prices for chat models work exactly as before.
